What it refuses to claim

A single node. A straight line. A nine-day-old file.

One data node
2 vCPU, 6 GB. There is no distribution clause in the DDL, so the benchmark wins are columnar scan, pushdown, pre-aggregation and the result cache — not MPP.
A straight line
The forecast is a 12-month slope extended linearly. It is not a model, and the .ics description says so in the event body.
A nine-day-old file
The data lags today by nine days. The Trust page prints 2026-10-22 instead of printing the word “live”.
104 regex patterns
Not a neural network, and unable to learn. That is the point: a ward officer can read the rule that fired on their own complaint.
Generated, not measured
The 288,768 readings come from 30_generate_readings.sql — deterministic and synthetic. The pipeline is real; the water is not.
One machine, one process
FastAPI, the Vite build and Exasol Personal on one laptop. Nothing here was tested under load or across nodes.
Every caveat in this video is printed inside the product — not in a slide.
