Why it happens · three clocks

Water fails first. The evidence arrives weeks later.

A municipal water supply gives three signals on three completely different clocks. Every dashboard in the building reports the last one — the only one that is already too late.
Clock 1 · hours
The probe moves

Turbidity doubles at 11 pm and residual chlorine decays below 0.2 mg/L. The instrument knows the same night.

Clock 2 · 7–14 days
The lab confirms

Coliform plates need incubation and a technician. Confirmation is a report, not an alarm.

Clock 3 · weeks
The clinic fills

Diarrhoeal cases show up in PHC registers after the exposure window has already closed.

instrument knowledge the 3-week window where an intervention still changes the outcome human cost
The window this product exists for

Between the probe firing and the clinic filling there are about three weeks in which an intervention still changes the outcome. Nothing in the municipal stack is built to act inside that window — and that window is the product.

WARDQ does not wait for clock three. It reports clock one, prices clock two, and turns “this ward is getting worse” into a dated work order:

Said out loud

The limits, before you ask.

Every number in this deck was measured on this machine. The three things that were not are below — and each one is a scope boundary, not a surprise.
The readings are generated
288,768 samples and 15,063 complaints are synthesised in pure SQL, with the monsoon intrusions and probe faults deliberately planted so the detectors have something true to find. The pipeline, the SQL and the detectors are real; the water is not.
One node, no replication
Exasol Personal on 2 vCPU / 6 GB. Fast enough to prove the columnar case; not a municipal HA story. Nothing here is benchmarked against a distributed cluster, and we say so on the benchmark page.
The forecast is a straight line, on purpose
OLS slope over a trailing 12 months with a widening 90-day band — no hydrology model, no rainfall covariates. It is a triage instrument that will be wrong in interesting ways, which is why the band and the confidence label ship with the number.
3% of readings are unusable
253 of 8,448 cells have no result. They are excluded from means, counted in completeness, and displayed on the Trust tab. A detector that pretends to 100% coverage is lying about the network.
What a pilot would need from a real ULB

Ninety days of probe and lab exports for one ward, the ward population table, and the WhatsApp group the complaints already arrive in. No integration project, no procurement cycle — the ingest path is a folder and a column map.
